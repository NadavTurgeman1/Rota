# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners, shift managers, and employees use Rota every day, with equal weight. An owner runs the business. A shift manager builds and publishes the weekly schedule for their sites. An employee clocks in and out, submits constraints, and reads the published schedule.

## Product Purpose

Rota is the weekly shift schedule for a business with sites and staff. It exists so the week can be built, checked, published, and lived: constraints, assignments, trades, and clock-in and clock-out. Success is a week that is legal for the business, readable by the people on it, and recorded when they arrive and leave.

## Positioning

The schedule, the constraints, and the attendance record are one product. A punch is the server time plus the phone's GPS fix, stored apart from the shifts. Weeks start on Sunday. The same account speaks Hebrew and English.

## Operating Context

People use it in a browser, often on a phone at the site and on a laptop in the office. The week is the unit of work. A schedule is private until it is published. Employees see their own future shifts, not everyone else's, when they trade or give a shift away.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed: organizations and sites, roles (owner, shift manager, employee), weekly schedule starting Sunday, constraints and agreements, shift trades and giveaways, published versus unpublished weeks, Hebrew calendar toggle, clock-in and clock-out with a checked phone clock and GPS, owner and shift-manager attendance log.

The redesign keeps every function and every existing Hebrew and English string. It replaces only the look.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Rota. The wordmark and the browser icon stay the current logo. The brand blue stays #2B5BFF. Hebrew is the primary reading direction; English is the same product.

## Evidence on Hand

The running app, its copy in `src/i18n/copy.ts`, and the logo files `public/rota-logo.png`, `public/rota-logo-dark.png`, `src/app/icon.png`, and `src/app/apple-icon.png`. No testimonials, pricing, or customer claims are on hand; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- The week is the object. Everything else hangs off it.
- A person sees what they are allowed to act on, and nothing that would let them break someone else's week.
- Time that counts is the server's time. The phone is checked against it.
- The same control means the same thing for an owner, a manager, and an employee.
- Hebrew and English carry the same information.
